# DOTS Survivors support grounding

Evaluate whether a player-facing response is grounded in the published DOTS Survivors knowledge supplied with the case.

Score 1 when every game-specific claim is directly supported by a supplied article, uncertain information is identified as such, and the response does not promise a fix, save restoration, or account action without evidence. Score 0 if it invents mechanics, release details, known bugs, or an official commitment. A helpful refusal with a ticket suggestion scores 1 when no article answers the question.
